What the recordings establish

Three named individual runs with original mass, motor limits and force/body guards. No parcel binding, runtime pose reset or rescue force. Complete-rate measurement projections and source hashes are included.

What comes next

  • Continuous six-parcel delivery is still unaccepted.
  • Configurable N-dog operation is the goal; current physical runtime is two.
  • Onboard localization, larger fleets and long-term multi-batch reliability remain future work.
